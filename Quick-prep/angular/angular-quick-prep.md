# Angular Interview Quick Prep: 7 Days

**Audience:** Backend developers who need Angular terminology and enough practical understanding to work on ordinary application tasks. This is not an expert or framework-specialist course. For current behavior, consult the [Angular documentation](https://angular.dev/overview).

| Day | Interview focus | Lecture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Components, standalone/NgModule structure, CLI | [Components and structure](day-01-components-and-structure.md) |
| 2 | Templates, interpolation, binding, events, control flow, pipes | [Templates and binding](day-02-templates-and-binding.md) |
| 3 | Services, DI scope, signals, Observable terminology | [Services and reactivity](day-03-services-and-observables.md) |
| 4 | Routes, outlets, parameters, lazy loading, guards | [Routing](day-04-routing-and-navigation.md) |
| 5 | Reactive/template forms, validation, HttpClient, interceptors | [Forms and HTTP](day-05-forms-and-http.md) |
| 6 | Lifecycle, change detection, signals, testing, debugging | [Lifecycle and debugging](day-06-lifecycle-and-debugging.md) |
| 7 | Feature flow, testing, version awareness, backend boundary | [Feature review](day-07-interview-practice.md) |

Lessons use brief numbered topic definitions and finish with grouped `Tricky points`. **Out of scope:** advanced RxJS operator design, framework internals, and specialist change-detection optimization.
